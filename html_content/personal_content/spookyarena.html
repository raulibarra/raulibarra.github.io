Spooky Arena

2.5D Online Multiplayer Arena Combat
Focus: Multiplayer Networking & Audio
Tech: Unity, C#, Photon Quantum
What I Built:
  • Multiplayer Arena Combat: Created a 6-player arena with wave-based spawning, real-time combat mechanics, and balanced platform placement for competitive gameplay.
  • Photon Quantum Integration: Implemented deterministic netcode for movement, projectiles, scoring, and respawns with low-latency synchronization across all players.
  • Dynamic Difficulty System: Built a wave system that progressively increases challenge while awarding points for destroyed objects and player eliminations.
  • Adaptive Audio System: Designed dynamic music transitions that respond to gameplay intensity, plus composed original soundtrack and immersive sound effects.
Skills Demonstrated: Advanced multiplayer networking, deterministic physics, competitive game balance, and interactive audio programming.

Fast-paced 6-player arena combat where strategy meets reflexes. Battle waves of enemies while competing against other players in this intense multiplayer experience.
