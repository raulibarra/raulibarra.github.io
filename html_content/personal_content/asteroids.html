Asteroids New Hope

Enhanced Asteroids with D.O.T.S. Performance System
Focus: Performance Optimization & D.O.T.S.
Tech: Unity, C#, D.O.T.S., ECS
What I Built:
  • High-Performance D.O.T.S. System: Implemented Unity's Data-Oriented Technology Stack for asteroid and projectile movement, achieving optimal performance with hundreds of entities.
  • Dynamic Weapon Upgrades: Created a progression system with randomized weapon enhancements from UFO destruction, including fire rate boosts and multi-shot capabilities.
  • Optimized Audio Management: Built a pooled audio system for efficient SFX playback and composed original music with responsive audio triggers for game events.
  • Smart Object Pooling: Implemented Unity's object pooling for bullets and effects, significantly reducing garbage collection and maintaining smooth performance.

Composed original soundtrack and designed immersive sound effects to enhance the classic arcade experience.

Skills Demonstrated: Advanced Unity optimization, D.O.T.S./ECS architecture, performance profiling, and scalable system design.

Classic space shooter with modern enhancements. Collect weapon upgrades from UFOs and experience smooth performance even with hundreds of asteroids on screen.
