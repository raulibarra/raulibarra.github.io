Tech:
Unity C# Mecanim Wwise Google Resonance
Key Features
  • Modular Character System: Architected a flexible character framework supporting stats, weapon mechanics, and AI behaviors for both player and enemies.
  • Complex Animation: Implemented Mecanim-driven animation controllers with synchronized footstep SFX, attack logic, and damage reactions.
  • Intelligent Enemy AI: Developed behavior systems for enemy pathfinding, combat engagement, and terrain-aware movement across 5 biomes.
  • Interactive Audio: Engineered terrain-detection footsteps, material-based weapon impacts, and Wwise-powered adaptive music.
  • Biome-Based Design: Created 5 open-world environments with spatial audio using Google Resonance and time-of-day cycles.
