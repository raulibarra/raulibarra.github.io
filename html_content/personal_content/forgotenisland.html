Echoes of the Forgotten Isle

Third-Person Open-World Prototype with Adaptive Audio
Focus: Character Systems & AI Programming
Tech: Unity, C#, Mecanim, Wwise, Google Resonance
What I Built:
  • Modular Character System: Architected a flexible character framework supporting stats, weapon mechanics, and AI behaviors for both player and enemies with reusable component-based design.
  • Complex Animation State Machines: Implemented Mecanim-driven animation controllers with synchronized footstep SFX, attack logic, damage reactions, and death sequences for fluid character interactions.
  • Intelligent Enemy AI: Developed behavior systems for enemy pathfinding, combat engagement, and terrain-aware movement across 5 distinct biomes with unique environmental interactions.
  • Interactive Audio Systems: Engineered terrain-detection footsteps, material-based weapon impacts, and Wwise-powered adaptive music transitions between exploration and combat states inspired by The Witcher 3.
  • Biome-Based World Design: Created 5 open-world environments (coast, plains, waterfalls, tundra, lake) with unique ambient soundscapes, spatial audio using Google Resonance, and time-of-day audio cycles.

Composed original neofolk orchestral score with real-time modal shifts and designed all environmental sound effects.

Skills Demonstrated: Advanced animation programming, modular architecture design, AI behavior systems, and interactive audio integration.
