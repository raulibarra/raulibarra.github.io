Degen Rivals

Multiplayer Vehicle Combat Game | WebGL & Mobile (iOS, Android)
Role: Senior Game Developer (Contractor)
Company: Tomorrow Media / Tevaera
Team Size:5+ including 3 devs
Tech: Unity 6.2, C#, Photon Quantum, ECS, Addressables
Key Contributions:
  • Weapons System: Built the entire ECS-based multiplayer weapons system using Photon Quantum. Had to handle fire rates, burst patterns, and ammo management while keeping everything synced between players.
  • Ability System: Engineered an ability system that drove 4 weapon effects (damage, stat changes) and 7 pickup effects like damage multipliers, speed boosts, shields, health regeneration, etc.
  • Car Audio: Used a pitch curve technique to simulate realistic gear shifts with just one audio source per car, keeping memory usage low while maintaining audio quality.
  • Audio Pipeline: Created a sound manager that handled multiple variations, randomization, and Addressable loading specifically optimized for WebGL builds.
  • WebGL Performance: Implemented audio compression and object pooling to ensure smooth browser performance across different devices.
  • Sound Design: Designed and implemented 15+ weapon sound effects and 10+ environmental audio interactions.
