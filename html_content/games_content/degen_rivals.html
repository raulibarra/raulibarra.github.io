Degen Rivals

Multiplayer Vehicle Combat Game | WebGL & Mobile (iOS, Android)
Role: Senior Game Developer (Contractor)
Company: Tomorrow Media / Tevaera
Duration: 4 months | Team: 4 developers
Tech: Unity 6.2, C#, Photon Quantum, ECS, Addressables
Key Contributions:
  • Weapons System: Built ECS-based multiplayer weapons with Photon Quantum - fire rates, burst patterns, ammo management.
  • Ability System: Engineered an ability system that was used to drive 15+ weapon effects (damage, stat changes) and 10+ pickup effects (damage 2x, increased speed, shield, hp regen, etc).
  • Car Audio: Realistic engine sounds using pitch curves and single audio sources - cut memory usage by 50%+.
  • Audio Pipeline: Sound manager with variations, randomization, and Addressable loading for WebGL optimization.
  • WebGL Performance: Audio compression and object pooling for smooth browser performance.
  • Sound Design: Created 15+ weapon SFX and 10+ environmental audio effects.
